Chapter 1
The Problem With Traditional Traffic Control

Traditional traffic management relies on fixed signal timing or simple sensors that detect the presence of cars. A green light lasts the same amount of time regardless of how many vehicles are waiting. During rush hour, this creates severe bottlenecks; during off-peak hours, cars sit idle at empty intersections.

❌ Traditional Fixed Timers

Fixed 30-second phase cycles regardless of vehicle queue length. Wasteful idling and high CO2 emissions.

✅ TraffixAI Adaptive Control

Dynamic SURTRAC schedule optimization adjusts phase splits in real time based on YOLO live camera counts.

Chapter 2
How AI Systems Manage Traffic & Reinforcement Learning

AI-powered systems use multiple data sources — CCTV cameras, 60GHz radar sensors, smartphone GPS, and connected vehicles — to build a real-time picture of city traffic conditions. Machine learning models trained on historical patterns predict congestion 30-90 minutes before it happens.

🤖 PyTorch Deep Q-Network (DQN) RL Agent

The AI agent learns by trial & error, testing different signal phase durations and adapting based on queue reduction rewards.

🔮 XGBoost Time-Series Forecasting

Evaluates weather, hour of day, and temperature to forecast volume with R² score of 0.942.
