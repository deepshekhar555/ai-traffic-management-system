What must be validated next

Report these outcomes only after controlled, reproducible testing with documented methods and ground truth.

01
Select a permitted test junction and document the observation window.
02
Compare vehicle counts and queue estimates with manual ground truth.
03
Compare timing proposals with a fixed-time baseline under the same demand.
04
Report latency, errors, uncertainty and test conditions.
05
Review camera permissions, privacy, fail-safe behavior and operator override.
06
Publish no performance claim until results are independently reproducible.

Proposed evaluation workflow

A development sequence for a controlled prototype; it is not a description of a deployed traffic-control service.

1
Obtain authorized video
Use permissioned test footage or a documented camera source.
2
Validate detections
Compare sample detections with annotated ground truth.
3
Estimate traffic state
Measure counts and occupancy by approach; calibrate before queue-distance estimates.
4
Generate a proposal
Apply documented phase, clearance and safety constraints.
5
Review as an operator
Keep human review and override; do not connect to a live junction.
6
Evaluate outcomes
Compare against a baseline and publish measured results with limitations.
